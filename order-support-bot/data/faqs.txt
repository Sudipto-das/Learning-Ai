Q: COD available hai kya?
A: Haan, COD available hai orders up to ₹2000 tak. ₹40 COD handling charge lagega. ₹2000 se zyada orders ke liye prepaid payment required hai.

Q: Kitne din mein delivery hogi?
A: Metro cities mein 3-5 business days, non-metro areas mein 5-7 business days. Agar express delivery chahiye toh ₹99 extra lagega aur 1-2 business days mein mil jayega.

Q: Return policy kya hai?
A: Delivery ke 7 din ke andar return kar sakte ho. Item unused hona chahiye aur original tags lage hone chahiye. Refund 3-5 business days mein process hota hai.

Q: Exchange kar sakte hain kya?
A: Haan, 7 din ke andar exchange ho sakta hai. Same product ka different size ya color mein exchange kar sakte ho. Agar replacement out of stock hai toh full refund mil jayega.

Q: Warranty hai kya products pe?
A: Electronics items pe 6-month manufacturer warranty hai. Footwear aur bags pe 30-day quality guarantee hai manufacturing defects ke against.

Q: Payment ke options kya hain?
A: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Wallets, aur Cash on Delivery (orders up to ₹2000) available hai.

Q: Free shipping hai kya?
A: ₹499 se zyada orders pe free standard shipping hai. ₹499 se kam orders pe shipping charges lagenge.

Q: Order cancel kar sakte hain kya?
A: Order place karne ke 2 hours ke andar cancel kar sakte ho. Dispatch ke baad cancel nahi hoga — delivery refuse kar sakte ho ya return initiate kar sakte ho.

Q: Kaise track karun apna order?
A: Order dispatch hone ke baad WhatsApp pe tracking link mil jayega. Aap hamari WhatsApp support pe bhi message kar sakte ho order status ke liye.

Q: Size kaise choose karun?
A: Hamari sizing guide mein apna bust aur waist measure karo. S (34" bust, 28" waist) se XXL (42" bust, 36" waist) tak sizes available hain. Confusion ho toh WhatsApp pe message karo — help karenge.

Q: Kya saare products pe return milega?
A: Zyaada products pe return available hai. Lekin innerwear, swimwear, aur customized products non-returnable hain.

Q: Delivery charge kitna hai?
A: ₹499 se upar orders pe free delivery hai. Express delivery ke liye ₹99 extra charge hai jo 1-2 din mein delivery karta hai.

Q: Points ya reward system hai kya?
A: Haan! Har ₹10 pe 1 point milta hai. 100 points = ₹50 discount next order pe. Points delivery ke baad credited hote hain aur 12 months valid hain.

Q: WhatsApp pe kaise contact karun?
A: Hamara WhatsApp number hai +91-9876543210. Monday se Saturday, 10 AM se 8 PM tak available hain. Sunday ko limited support hai.

Q: Mera order damage aa gaya hai kya karun?
A: Turant WhatsApp pe photo ke saath message karo 48 hours ke andar. Hum replacement ya refund arrange karenge — aapko return shipping charge nahi dena padega.

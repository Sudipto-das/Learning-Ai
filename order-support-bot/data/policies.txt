## Return Policy
- 7-day return window from the date of delivery
- Item must be unused, unwashed, and with original tags attached
- Return request must be raised through WhatsApp or email within 7 days
- Refund is processed within 3-5 business days after we receive the returned item
- Shipping charges are non-refundable (unless the item was defective or wrong item was sent)
- Certain items like innerwear, swimwear, and customized products are non-returnable
- Damaged or defective items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos

## Exchange Policy
- Exchange is available within 7 days of delivery
- Exchange is allowed for different size or color of the same product
- If the desired replacement is out of stock, a full refund will be initiated
- One-time exchange per order is allowed

## Shipping Policy
- Free standard shipping on all orders above ₹499
- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days (metro cities), 5-7 business days (non-metro)
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days at an additional charge of ₹99
- We ship across all pin codes in India via our delivery partners
- Order tracking link is shared on WhatsApp once the order is dispatched
- Orders placed before 2 PM are dispatched the same day (business days)

## COD (Cash on Delivery) Policy
- COD is available for orders with total value up to ₹2000
- A COD handling charge of ₹40 applies to all COD orders
- COD is not available for orders above ₹2000 — prepaid payment is required
- Payment can be made via cash or UPI at the doorstep
- If the customer is not available at delivery, one reattempt will be made
